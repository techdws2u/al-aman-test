How It Works

A guided journey — from wishes to a plan that holds.

Islamic Legacy Planning shouldn't feel like paperwork. Here's how Al-Aman walks you through it — with real people and real oversight behind every recommendation.

Every Muslim family's legacy plan is built from the same tradition, but no two plans are the same. A Wasiat honours certain wishes. A Hibah honours others. Sometimes one is enough. Sometimes both are needed. Knowing which is right for your family — and drafting it properly — is the work.

Al-Aman was built to make that work clear, considered, and yours. Here's how we walk you through it.

01

Tell us about you

We start with a conversation, not a form. Who's in your family. What you've built. What you'd like to happen when you're no longer here to say it yourself. There are no wrong answers — only enough clarity to know what your plan actually needs.

02

See your two paths

We explain — clearly, in your own language — what a Wasiat can do for your situation, and where Hibah comes in. Not as two separate products, but as two tools in one plan. You'll understand exactly what each covers, and when both are needed.

03

Build your plan, guided

You choose what fits your family — a Wasiat alone, or a Wasiat with Hibah. We draft the documents with you, step by step, checking every decision against the Shariah framework designed by Sarah Ali & Co. No jargon. No blind spots. If something doesn't sit right, we pause.

04

Sign, declare, and rest easy

Once your plan is ready, you complete two things. First, the witnessed signing of your printed Wasiat. Then, your Lafaz Qabul — a recorded declaration that gives your Wasiat its proper weight. As part of your plan, Global Asset Trustee is appointed as your backup executor (at no cost), stepping in if your primary executor is unable or unwilling to serve. Nothing hangs on a shelf. Nothing waits to be found. Your plan is live from the day you finish.

When the day comes — and it will, for all of us — your family shouldn't be guessing while they are grieving. Your wishes will already be there, in black and white. A professional trustee stands by as your backup executor (wasiyy). This is the legacy worth leaving.
