How It Works

A guided journey — from wishes to a plan that holds.

Islamic estate planning shouldn't feel like paperwork. Here's how Al-Aman walks you through it — step by step, with real people and real oversight behind every recommendation.

The Situation

Why a Wasiat alone is often not enough.

Most Malaysian Muslims who plan for their families think they need one thing: a Wasiat. And they're partly right — a Wasiat is essential. But under Shariah, a Wasiat can only distribute up to one-third of what you leave behind, and only to non-heirs — people who wouldn't already receive a share under Faraid.

That leaves a real gap. If you want to provide for your own spouse or children beyond what Faraid allocates, or if you want to give more than one-third to a cause close to your heart, a Wasiat alone cannot honour those wishes. This is where Hibah comes in — a gift given during your lifetime, sitting outside Faraid, without the one-third limit.

Al-Aman was built because most providers still hand you a Wasiat and stop there. We think you deserve to understand both tools — and to have a plan that uses them together.

The Journey

Four steps, at your own pace.

You don't have to figure it all out in one sitting. Al-Aman is designed to let you pause, think, come back, and adjust.

01

Understand your situation

A short guided conversation about your family, your assets, and what you'd like to happen. No jargon. No pressure. Just enough to know what your plan actually needs.

02

See your two paths

We show you clearly how Wasiat and Hibah apply to your situation — what each can and can't cover, and how they work together. No surprises later.

03

Build your plan

Draft your Wasiat and (if it fits your needs) your Hibah documents online, guided step by step. Every choice is checked against the Shariah framework designed by Sarah Ali & Co.

04

Sign, register, and rest easy

Complete the required signing and witnessing steps. Your documents are securely registered, and Global Asset Trustee stands ready as your professional executor when the time comes.

The Outcome

What you actually end up with.

Not a stack of papers you'll never look at again. A clear, working plan that speaks for you — quietly and correctly — when your family needs it most.

Your Islamic Will, properly witnessed, professionally drafted, and safely stored.

Your Hibah documents (if needed)

Where a Hibah is right for your situation, the appropriate documents drafted and executed.

A named professional executor

When The Time Comes

What actually happens.

When you pass, your family is often at their most vulnerable. Bureaucratic complications shouldn't add to that weight. Because Global Asset Trustee has been appointed as your professional executor from day one, they take on the administrative work — locating your Wasiat, applying for probate, contacting beneficiaries, and distributing your estate according to your wishes and Shariah.

For Hibah documents executed during your lifetime, those transfers have already been completed — the assets sit outside your estate and outside Faraid, exactly as you intended.

Your family doesn't have to figure out where to start, which office to visit, or how to interpret your intentions. That work was done — quietly, correctly — when you set your plan up.
